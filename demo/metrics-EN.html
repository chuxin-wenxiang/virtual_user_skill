Key Behavioral Insights

Insight 1: 88VIP Members Are Natural Early Adopters

88VIP users show significantly higher intention for both click (+18.8pp) and conversion (+14.8pp) versus regular users. Key drivers include habitual platform trust built through long-term usage, lower price sensitivity (reducing the "comparison shopping" friction), and the membership-driven mindset of "this is my exclusive benefit." Recommendation: prioritize 88VIP as the Phase 1 rollout audience.

Insight 2: Click ≠ Convert — A Clear Trust Gap

The funnel drop from 43.5% CTR to 16.1% CVR represents a 63% decay. The barrier is not "unwillingness to explore" but "unwillingness to commit." Three primary resistance factors: cross-platform price comparison habits (the platform is not the sole decision platform), uncertainty about AI recommendation quality ("what if it goes wrong"), and a perceived personalization gap (users believe AI can't understand their unique needs).

Insight 3: "Ad Fear" Is the Primary Barrier for Regular Users

Among regular users, "worried the recommendations are just ads / high-margin rooms" is the most frequently cited rejection reason. This reflects a fundamental distrust of platform-user interest alignment among non-VIP users. Feature design must visibly demonstrate recommendation logic (e.g., "based on your browsing history" rather than a black box) and provide transparent "why this room" explanations.

Insight 4: High Pain-Point Alignment Doubles Intent

Users in the scenario library who explicitly expressed "too many hotel options, can't decide" scored 40-60% higher in click intention compared to users who "already have clear preferences." This validates the feature's PMF hypothesis — the core value scenario is "choice overload" rather than universal need. Product should target entry-point display conditions precisely (e.g., show only when room types > 5).
